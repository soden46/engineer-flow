# Family Taxonomy

Use a small stable family set. Do not over-fragment.

Initial families:

- `debugging`
- `testing`
- `security`
- `architecture`
- `database`
- `performance`
- `api-integration`
- `frontend-ui`
- `infrastructure-devops`
- `data-processing`
- `code-quality-refactoring`
- `planning-execution`
- `documentation`
- `dependency-tooling`
- `ai-llm-engineering`
- `version-control-review`
- `memory-infrastructure`
- `meta-orchestration`
- `other`

Family classification happens before skill selection. Candidate skills are then restricted to compatible families.

Framework-specific skills should keep their domain family while recording frameworks. Example: `laravel-database-optimization` belongs to `performance` and `database`, with framework `laravel`.
